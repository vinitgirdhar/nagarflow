# NagarFlow product film

The film follows the supplied 45-second narration and the central idea that completion requires
reviewed evidence. White framing, Inter typography, restrained blue accents and quiet transitions
surround recordings of the actual NagarFlow application.

## Sources and scope

- Actual local Next.js and Flask application; no application source changes or intercepted API responses.
- Isolated synthetic complaints, teams, vehicles and historical records. No customer/resident records.
- Real authenticated actions: complaint classification, linked work-order creation, team assignment,
  vehicle dispatch acceptance, task photograph upload, submission, supervisor approval and document search.
- The recorded work order ended in `COMPLETED`, linked to sample complaint `NF-1042`, with team `A-01`.
- Vehicle dispatch remained `EN_ROUTE`. The film does not imply GPS tracking or confirmed arrival.
- Forecasts show the actual two-day table, not an invented chart or validated field accuracy.
- Search shows the real citation to a fictional approved sanitation checklist.
- A generated photograph marked SAMPLE IMAGE demonstrates evidence upload. It is not evidence of
  municipal work. Every product scene is labeled DEMO · SAMPLE DATA.
- Map credit: © OpenStreetMap contributors, retained in the film framing.

## Audio and rendering

- Supplied script, spoken with synthetic Indian English voice `en-IN-PrabhatNeural`.
- Scene-level timing adjustments preserve all words within the requested 45 seconds.
- Original locally synthesized piano/synth score and quiet tonal effects; no stock music.
- Source mix measured at -16.19 LUFS integrated, -2.79 dBTP true peak.
- Final framing is native 3840 × 2160. Desktop recordings originated at 1600 × 1000;
  the mobile recording originated at 430 × 900. Product recordings are enlarged within the 4K layout.
- Settled product reveal is baked into frame zero as the share thumbnail.
- Captions are supplied separately and are not burned into the image.
