# NagarFlow | Official Product Launch Film

45 seconds · 3840 × 2160 · 30 fps · 16:9

## Idea

A complaint is not resolved when it is received. It is resolved when the work is verified.
NagarFlow connects municipal complaint review, work orders, crews, vehicles and human verification.
For municipal operators, field teams and supervisors. Its visual hook is the progression from three
separate civic issues into one accountable workflow. Tone: quiet, confident, precise.

## Production decisions

Use the actual local application, real authenticated sessions and isolated synthetic database records.
Never intercept APIs or fabricate application screens. Production application source stays unchanged.
Keep the application's existing warm palette and fonts inside recordings; film titles use Inter,
#111827 text, #2563EB accents and #F8FAFC canvas with white surfaces and fine #E2E8F0 borders.
Every product scene carries “DEMO · SAMPLE DATA”. Maps retain OpenStreetMap attribution.
People shown in the workspace are fictional role labels. No resident or customer data is used.
The evidence photograph is synthetic and labeled SAMPLE IMAGE, demonstrating upload mechanics only.

Current source deviations from the reference storyboard:
- Complaint review is a form, not a map. The genuine dashboard/dispatch map provides geographic context.
- Forecasts are a two-day table, not a line chart. Show the real table and its limits.
- Work orders and vehicle dispatch are separate controls; do not invent a vehicle-to-task relationship.
- Human actions create orders, assign teams, accept dispatch and approve completion.
